Privacy Policy

Clopos Track — GPS tracking app for field staff

Overview

Clopos Track is an internal work application of Clopos. It is intended solely for company employees and requires a company-issued account to sign in. The app is not intended for public use.

This document explains what data the app collects, why it is collected, and how it is protected.

Data we collect

DataPurpose
Location (GPS)
precise coordinates		 To determine where an employee is during working hours, to record site check-in and check-out, and to calculate the distance travelled.
Photos and videos Work reports (inspection reports, installation handover, site photos). Files are selected by the employee and sent to the corresponding Microsoft Teams work channel.
Employee name and ID To identify who submitted each report or event.
Device information
model, Android version, battery level		 To investigate technical issues and keep the app running reliably.
Crash reports To detect and fix failures in the app.

Background location use

The app collects location in the background only while a work shift is open — that is, even when the app is closed or the phone is locked.

This is essential to the core purpose of the app: an employee's work route must be recorded without interruption so that site visits and working time are calculated correctly.

  • Tracking runs only while a shift is open
  • Location collection stops as soon as the employee ends the shift
  • While tracking is active, a persistent notification is shown in the status bar — the employee always knows tracking is running
  • No location data is collected outside working hours

Transparency

This app is provided officially by the company and is not a covert surveillance tool.

  • Employees are informed that tracking takes place
  • The app icon is visible and is never hidden
  • A persistent notification is displayed the entire time tracking is active
  • Tracking runs only while a work shift is open and stops the moment it is closed
  • Signing in requires a company-issued account

Data sharing

Data is never sold to third parties and is never used for advertising.

Data is processed only by the following services:

  • Clopos servers — storage of work data
  • Microsoft Teams / SharePoint — work reports and photos submitted by the employee
  • Google Firebase — delivery of push notifications
  • Sentry — technical crash reports

Security and retention

  • All data transmission is encrypted over HTTPS
  • Access is limited to authorised management staff
  • Work history is retained in line with the company's internal policy
  • When an employee leaves, the account is deactivated and the app's access to the server is revoked immediately

Employee rights

Employees may request information about their data and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. To do so, please contact company management or use the address below.
